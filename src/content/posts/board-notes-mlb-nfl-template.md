Board notes are **field reports on a price**, not recommendations. They answer: what was the number, where did it go, what is the best explanation, and what is still unknown? They do not answer “who do I bet?”

Copy the skeleton. Replace the hypotheticals. If a sentence sounds like a lock, cut it. House rules: [style guide](../../style-guide/). How to read the tape: [Reading a line move](../reading-a-line-move/).

## Skeleton (use on every card)

```
Sport / game / start (ET)
Open → current → close (if posted)
Move type: steam / grind / juice-only / bounce
Best explanation: news / money / follow / unknown
Who followed: one shop vs the market
Limits / late-thin?: yes/no / unknown
What would change this note
What this note is not: a pick
```

Keep examples **hypothetical** unless you are citing a historical board. Round numbers. No “play,” “lock,” or unit talk.

## Sample MLB card (hypothetical)

**Blue Jays at Red Sox · night game · Fenway**  
*Hypothetical board. Not a live number. Not a wager.*

| | Run line | Total | Moneyline (approx.) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Open** | TOR −1.5 (+150) / BOS +1.5 (−170) | 8.5 (−110/−110) | TOR −120 / BOS +100 |
| **Current** | TOR −1.5 (+135) / BOS +1.5 (−155) | 9 (−105/−115) | TOR −135 / BOS +115 |
| **Close** | *not yet* | *not yet* | *not yet* |

**Move type:** grind on the moneyline and a half-run walk-up on the total; juice on the run line tightened against Boston.

**Best explanation (news-led, still incomplete):** Boston’s listed starter was a game-time decision at open. Afternoon reports say he is **out**; a bullpen game is the working assumption. Totals often tick up when a rotation arm comes off a night card. The moneyline grind toward Toronto is the same story told in a different market. **Unconfirmed:** how many innings the replacement mix is actually expected to cover, and whether Toronto’s starter is still on a pitch count.

**Who followed:** two major books walked the total to 9 within an hour of each other; a third held 8.5 and lengthened juice on the over. That is partial follow, not a full-market steam.

**Limits / late-thin:** afternoon still; limits look normal. Revisit after first-pitch weather (wind out to left would be a *different* note).

**What would change this note**

- Starter is reinstated; expect the total and moneyline to bounce toward the open.
- Toronto starter is scratched too; then this explanation is garbage — say so.
- Total goes to 9 everywhere and juice equalizes: more complete follow.

**What this note is not.** It is not “take the over” or “take the Jays.” It is a description of a board reacting to a probable scratch. CLV, if you care later, is against the *close*, not against this paragraph.

## Sample NFL card (hypothetical)

**Lions at Packers · Sunday afternoon · outdoor**  
*Hypothetical board. Not a live number. Not a wager.*

| | Spread | Total | Moneyline (approx.) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Open** | DET +2.5 (−110) / GB −2.5 (−110) | 47.5 (−110/−110) | DET +120 / GB −140 |
| **Current** | DET +3 (−105) / GB −3 (−115) | 46.5 (−110/−110) | DET +130 / GB −150 |
| **Close** | *not yet* | *not yet* | *not yet* |

**Move type:** juice-then-point on the spread (key 3); small grind down on the total. Not a one-minute steam.

**Best explanation (mixed; do not overfit):** Green Bay opened a short home favorite. Through the week the number walked to **−3 / −115**, which is a different product than −2.5 / −110 (you are paying more to lay the key number). Public graphics will say “everyone loves the pack at home.” Maybe. **Also on the table:** a mid-week listing of a Detroit skill player as questionable, still unofficial. Totals ticked down a point with no matching weather story yet.

**Who followed:** spread move visible at most shops; one book is still +2.5 −120 on Detroit (a hold / shade, not a separate universe). Totals are less unanimous: 46.5 and 47 both up.

**Limits / late-thin:** Wednesday board. Do not treat this like a Sunday 12:50 p.m. tick. Inactive lists will be a new note.

**What would change this note**

- Detroit player is **out** vs **full go** — those are two different spreads, not the same story with extra confidence.
- Number bounces back to +2.5 even juice: the walk to 3 may have been positioning, not information.
- Weather model actually drops (wind/snow): then the total grind has a news engine.

**Reverse-line temptation.** If a TV chart says the public is on Green Bay *and* the spread is moving toward Green Bay, that is not a reverse line move. If the public is on Green Bay and the spread moves toward Detroit, ask for handle, limits, and news before you recite the myth. See the line-move lesson.

**What this note is not.** Not “take Detroit +3.” Not “the close will be 3.5 so buy now.” If you later log CLV, log the price you *would have had* as a hypothetical — and label it hypothetical.

## How to file a note without becoming a tout

- **One engine per paragraph.** News, then money, then follow. Do not blend them into “sharps knew.”
- **Write the unknown in public.** Game-time decisions, inactives, wind, pitch counts.
- **Separate juice from the point.** −3 / −115 is not the same as −2.5 / −110.
- **Never close on a wager.** The last line is what would falsify the explanation, or a pointer to CLV after the close.
